Privacy notice

Updated 24 September 2026.

Who to contact

A To Z Home Improvement Ltd is responsible for the personal information you give us when you enquire about our services. Email Atozhomeimprovementuk@gmail.com or call 07424 940579 with any privacy questions.

Information used to arrange a visit

The enquiry form asks for your name, telephone number, postcode, the service you need and any optional details you choose to add about the job. If you contact us by email or telephone, we also receive the details you choose to share with us. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your enquiry.

We use these details to respond, discuss the work, check the location and arrange a suitable visit. Where you are asking us for a quote or to provide a service, this processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering a contract. Our legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries and keeping the website secure apply to other correspondence and security checks.

Services that handle your information

The website is hosted by Vercel. When the online form is available, Web3Forms processes the submission and sends it to our Gmail inbox. These providers may process technical information, including your IP address, to deliver their services and prevent abuse. The form uses hCaptcha for a security check.

You can read the providers’ information at Vercel, Web3Forms, Google and hCaptcha. Their services may process information outside the UK; their privacy information explains the locations and safeguards that apply.

How long information is kept

We retain enquiry correspondence while it is needed to respond, arrange the requested work and resolve related questions. If an enquiry becomes a job, relevant records may also be needed for accounting, legal obligations or a dispute. The applicable period depends on the purpose and those obligations; contact us for information about your particular enquiry.

Web3Forms states that form submissions are retained for up to three years unless a shorter period applies or they are deleted earlier. Copies delivered to our email inbox are separate from the form service’s records.

Cookies and website features

This website does not add advertising or analytics trackers. When you use an available online form, the security-check provider may use browser storage or cookies to protect against automated submissions. You can also contact us directly by telephone or email.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access, correct, erase or receive a copy of your personal information, restrict how it is used, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact us using the details above. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
